Swing Check

Your phone camera finds your body joints and shows how your hips, shoulders and hands move in a baseball swing.

For the best result
  • Stand the phone still (tripod, bag or fence) about 3 to 4 m away, at belt height.
  • Film from behind the batter or facing the batter, so both shoulders and both hips show.
  • Get the whole body in view, with good light and only one person in the picture.
  • Film through a gap in the cage net, or from outside the net's edge. Net in front of the camera confuses the tracker.
  • Use normal Video mode. Skip Slo-mo, it makes the timings wrong.
  • Keep it short: start recording just before the swing and stop right after. Only the first 10 seconds are checked.
